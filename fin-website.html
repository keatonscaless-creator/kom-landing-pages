Our Story

Why Fishing In Nebraska

I've been fishing Lake McConaughy for over 25 years — 20 first-place tournament finishes, 25 more in the top five. Somewhere in there I got tired of gear that couldn't keep up with a full day on the lake, so I started making my own.

FIN is still a small, family-run operation. Every drop is limited, every piece is made to actually get worn on the water — not just look good hanging in a closet.
