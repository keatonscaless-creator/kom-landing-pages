Our Story

Why Fishing In Nebraska

FIN started the way most good ideas do — on the water, complaining that nothing we were wearing actually held up to a full day of fishing. So we made our own.

It's still a small, family-run operation. Every drop is limited, every piece is made to actually get worn on the river — not just look good hanging in a closet.
