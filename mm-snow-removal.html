FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us.

When do you come out?

Once snowfall reaches 2 inches, you're automatically on the schedule. For overnight storms we start early so most driveways are clear before people leave for work. For storms that keep going all day, we may come back for a second pass.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Once you're on the route we just show up and clear everything. We'll text you when you're done.

Will the snowblower damage my driveway?

No. That's one of the biggest reasons homeowners choose a snowblower over a plow. There's no steel blade scraping your concrete, pavers or the edges of your lawn.

What about ice?

We clear down to the pavement, which helps a lot with refreezing. You can also add ice melt on your steps and walkways to any visit.

Can you handle gravel or paver driveways?

Yes. Let us know in your quote request and we'll adjust how we clear it so we don't throw gravel or damage pavers.

Do you do commercial properties?

We focus on homes, but we do take a limited number of commercial walkways and small lots. Mention it in your quote request and we'll let you know if we can fit you in.

How do I pay?

Seasonal plans are billed up front or in monthly installments, and per-storm service is billed after each visit. We'll send an invoice you can pay online.
