Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 10, 2026  |  HNS Property Services, 16102 Azalea Way, Parker, CO 80134

Information We Collect

When you submit an inspection or estimate request through one of our landing pages or through a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Instant Form, we collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), property address, and details about your project (e.g. roofing, storm damage, basement finishing, or kitchen remodeling). This information is used solely to contact you and provide an estimate or schedule a service.

How We Use Your Information

We use your contact information to:

  • Respond to your inspection or estimate request
  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Send appointment reminders, inspection confirmations, and project updates
  • Assist with insurance claim documentation, if applicable
  • Send SMS text messages, if you have opted in (see SMS Communications below)

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Communications

When you submit a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Instant Form or one of our landing page request forms, you may be given the option to opt in to receive SMS text messages from HNS Property Services. By checking that box or submitting the form with SMS consent enabled, you agree to receive text messages related to lead follow-up, appointment scheduling, and inspection confirmations. See our Terms of Service for full SMS program details. Consent to SMS is not required to receive an estimate or services, and you may still request an estimate without opting in.

Your SMS opt-in data (mobile phone number and consent status) will never be shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party or affiliate for any purpose, including marketing. SMS opt-in information is used exclusively by HNS Property Services to deliver the messages described above.

Cookies & Tracking

Our landing pages use the Meta Pixel and standard browser cookies to measure ad performance, understand which campaigns drive inspection requests, and improve our advertising. These tools may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. This tracking data is used for analytics and ad optimization purposes only and is not linked to your SMS opt-in status or shared with third parties beyond Meta's standard advertising platform functionality. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Data Security

Your information is stored securely in our CRM system, which uses industry-standard encryption, access controls, and authentication to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Access to your data is limited to HNS Property Services staff who need it to respond to your request or provide service.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe from SMS at any time by replying STOP to any text message from HNS Property Services, or by contacting us directly using the information below
  • Update your information (phone number, address, or project details) by contacting us directly
  • Request deletion of your personal information at any time

Opting out of SMS does not affect your ability to receive an estimate or services from HNS Property Services.
