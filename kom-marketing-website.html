Questions

Straight Answers

What platform do you run ads on?

Meta — Facebook and Instagram. Specifically using the Conversions objective with manual targeting and placements. No Advantage+ shortcuts that tank lead quality.

How quickly will I start seeing leads?

Most clients see their first leads within 48–72 hours of launch. The first 7 days is the learning phase — leads get more consistent and cheaper from there.

Do I need to provide my own creative?

Before/after photos or video clips of your work are ideal — but not required. We can build creative from what you have. The more real footage, the better.

What's the minimum ad spend you recommend?

$30–50/day is the sweet spot to get meaningful data and consistent leads. Less than that and the algorithm doesn't have enough signal to optimize.

What does the strategy call actually cover?

Your market, your current situation, what's working or not, and a specific roadmap for your account. You'll leave with a real plan — not a sales pitch.
